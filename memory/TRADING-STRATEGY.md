# Trading Strategy

Paper trading only.

## Portfolio Shape

- Core quality book: 50-60%.
- Momentum/catalyst book: 25-35%.
- Cash reserve: minimum 10-20%.
- Stocks/ETFs only.
- No options, crypto, margin, short selling, or live trading in v1.

## Decision Standard

Every paper trade must explain:

- business-quality thesis
- specific catalyst
- momentum evidence
- bear case
- stop or exit plan
